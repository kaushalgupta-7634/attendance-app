Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the ATTENDX system processes coordinates, account profiles, and logging parameters.

1. Geo-Location & Proximity Data

ATTENDX uses your device's GPS coordinates to verify that you are physically present inside the designated classroom during active checking sessions. We process this mathematical calculation (distance to target) dynamically on the server. We **never** store historical trace paths, nor do we track your device location when you are not actively signing in.

2. Data Security & Retention

User accounts, email profiles, and roster data are secured behind standard JSON Web Token (JWT) cryptographic signatures and industry-standard password hashing mechanisms. We retain check-in history records solely for academic evaluation by the designated faculty instruction team.
