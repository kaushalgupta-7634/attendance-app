About ATTENDX

ATTENDX is a modern, location-verified academic attendance and classroom management portal designed to eliminate attendance fraud, simplify student tracking, and enhance administrative efficiency.

Traditional attendance sheets and digital sign-ins are highly vulnerable to proxy check-ins (e.g., sharing class pins or signing in friends). Furthermore, instructors waste valuable classroom time managing manual lists. ATTENDX resolves this by requiring physical proximity verification via device GPS coordinates alongside encrypted, time-sensitive rotating QR tokens.

Created by senior educational technology developers, our mission is to build robust, secure, and intuitive tools that support modern educational institutions and allow instructors to focus on what matters most: teaching.
